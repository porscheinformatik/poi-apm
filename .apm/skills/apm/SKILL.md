This skill covers two domains: authoring APM packages (primitives, manifests, and configuration) and managing the APM CLI itself (installation, updates, versioning, and core commands).

## Quick navigation

- **Authoring APM packages**: Author source primitives under `.apm/` and `apm.yml`
- **Managing APM CLI**: Install, update, verify version, or configure APM itself
- **Core CLI commands**: `compile`, `install`, `audit`, `update`, `self-update`
